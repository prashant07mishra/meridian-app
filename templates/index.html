Leadership Profile

Leading With Purpose & Perspective

Utkarsh Kishore is a strategy, growth, and business transformation professional with over 15 years of experience helping multinational corporations and growth-stage businesses navigate change across Asia-Pacific and emerging markets. Alongside his corporate career, he has also built and scaled an entrepreneurial agri-asset venture.

He founded Meridian & Company with a simple belief: meaningful transformation extends beyond business outcomes. Sustainable success is achieved when individuals, organisations, and civilisation discover, nurture, and realise their potential. Guided by The Meridian Divine Light, the firm’s philosophy, Meridian combines strategy, transformation, and AI with a long-term perspective to help clients create enduring value.

Beyond his advisory and entrepreneurial work, Utkarsh is the author of The Divine Light Series, a collection of books exploring leadership, human potential, organisations, AI, and the long-term evolution of civilisation. His writing is guided by the same philosophy that underpins Meridian & Company.
